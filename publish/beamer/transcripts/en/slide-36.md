**Purpose.**
The briefing cites mechanisms and checkpoints; the appendix is the curated evidence store behind those claims. Three buckets: publication digests, commercial product signals, open repositories — tiered for the agentic-compiler prediction, not an exhaustive catalog of AI or compilers.

Mechanism first; demote headlines and generic forge AI. Update your roadmap stance only when this evidence moves, not when a blog post averages conflicting numbers.

**What follows.**
