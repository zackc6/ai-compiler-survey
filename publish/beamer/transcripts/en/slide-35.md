# Slide 35: Discussion

Plain closing slide — light background, no frametitle bar. On-slide: “Discussion” headline, three boxed questions, footer ask. Spoken: thank the room, reopen the central question, invite debate.

**Open.**
Thank them for the time. Restate the ask from slide 1: is the hybrid prediction (~2027–31) the right bet for *their* roadmap? Agents own search, orchestration, synthesis on the control plane; compilers own lowering, legality, measure, fallback on the data plane. Not LLM-as-compiler.

**Question 1 — which checkpoint flips investment first?**
C1 (Magellan vs MLGO default), C2 (median/p90 on pinned traces), or C9 (second-vendor TritorX-class coverage playbook)? Different orgs watch different falsifiers — infra teams may care C1; serving teams C2; hardware vendors C9. Ask which one would change their budget line.

**Question 2 — harder commercial bar: oracle strength or replay/freeze?**
Blockers one vs two. Some orgs have formal methods culture but weak artifact regression; others freeze well but admit too cheaply. Forces prioritization between T2/T6 and T3/T10.

**Question 3 — building job (a), (b), or (d) first — what do you refuse online?**
(a) online propose→measure, (b) offline heuristic evolution, (d) bring-up coverage→performance. Online has latency and safety constraints; offline needs review capacity; bring-up needs sim/silicon oracles. What they *refuse* to run online reveals their real risk model.

**Footer beat.**
Watch settlement signals — do not average disagreements. Tier A products, ★ digests, and public traces move the prediction; opinions do not.

Close cleanly. Point to appendix for evidence depth if questions go technical.
