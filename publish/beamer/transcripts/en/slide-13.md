# Slide 13: Merge ≠ replace — three levels

Answer the perception trap: “When does everything merge so agents *replace* the AI compiler?” Three levels — **do not conflate them**. This is **M1≠M3** and the settled **C6-B** lean.

**M1 — Soft merge (Horizon A → B, green).**
One **e2e** controller + admit/trace bus over band *tools* — slides 11–12 architecture. Data plane stays; classical lowering, legality, fallback intact. Agents do **not** replace the compiler; they merge the *optimizer* under **F**. **Predicted path.**

**M2 — Search-surface merge (amber, contested / partial ~2029–31).**
Fewer agent-training IRs if conflict **C4** settles — maybe one canonical agent-facing surface per vendor family. Lowers remain **multi-band** L1–L7; still hybrid. Partial consolidation of *search*, not deletion of execution substrate.

**M3 — Hard replace (ember, NOT claimed thru Horizon B ~2031).**
Default production path with **no** classical admit/fallback — agents *are* the compiler. Only if **C6-A** settles (free rewrite wins everywhere with strong oracles). Survey explicitly does **not** bet on this through ~2031.

**Perception trap (bottom ink box).**
“I only talk to an agent” is often **M1 + freeze** — classical lowering still ran under admit in build-**CI**; the UX hides bands, not removes them. Ask what happens when admit fails.

**Lean — A8 / C6-B.**
Merge the optimizer under **F**; do not delete the execution substrate. Say it twice if the room is vendor-heavy on “agent-native compiler” marketing.

Closing beat: M1 is the bet; M3 is the falsifier. Bridge to slide 14: what actually ships by 2028 under M1.
