Technique-to-checkpoint map. On-slide: band per conflict ID with arrow from techniques. Spoken: for each checkpoint, name the techniques and what “settled” looks like — hybrid lean throughout.

Needs coverage→performance agents plus dialect sinks and a second-vendor TritorX-class public playbook. SKU story: coverage SLA (Service Level Agreement) first, performance SLA second.

**C10 ← T5 — codesign vs auto tape-out.**
Needs ISA/dialect *proposals only* — humans and chip-design tools own tape-out. Agents stress kernels and IR today; autonomous microarch is not claimed.

Closing line: each checkpoint is falsifiable. Map your roadmap investments to T-rows; do not fund techniques that do not connect to a checkpoint you are willing to watch.
