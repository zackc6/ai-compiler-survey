# Slide 2: Agenda

Walk the six beats on the vertical spine — left to right in importance for *this* room, not slide order.

**1 — Trends · Keep substrate.**
Open with §1 backdrop: two stacks converging (compilers for AI vs AI for compilers), six active trends, then the preserve/adopt split. Message: next-gen ≠ throw away LLVM/MLIR — make classical stacks **agent-addressable**. Budget ~5 minutes unless the room is cold on compiler basics.

**2 — Verdict + architecture.**
This is the weight of the briefing. Executive verdict (control plane vs data plane), four agent jobs (a–d), target stack diagram, architecture evolution across Horizon A/B, then the four architecture slides: **multi-band L1–L7** data plane, why one universal cost model fails Horizon A, **e2e-optimal-seeking under product fitness F**, and **M1≠M3** merge levels. Do not rush these — they are the spine the rest hangs on.

**3 — Roadmap checkpoints.**

**4 — Commercial blockers + gaps.**
Top five productization gaps before research wishlist — oracles, cost/replay, agent↔compiler contract, distributional evidence, ownership. Each blocker gets a dedicated slide; lean from SURVEY §5.7.

**5 — Technical prediction (techniques).**
In/out-of-compiler techniques, checkpoint map — accelerators for the roadmap, not a second narrative. Compress if time is tight.

**6 — Stance · Ask · Appendix.**
Close with explicit stance, restate the title ask, appendix for depth. If short on time, cut appendix first, then compress §5 techniques — never cut verdict + architecture + checkpoints.
