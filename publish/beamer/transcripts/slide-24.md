# Slide 24: Technical Prediction — Within The Compiler

Walk five in-compiler techniques and what is critically missing:

1. Typed agent↔compiler interfaces — missing standard schemas and vendor-neutral tool servers; unlocks C3/C5/C6.
2. Admit/fallback machinery — missing shared reject-and-restore policy; unlocks hybrid C6 and money-grade admit.
3. Control files, hints, fingerprints — missing content-addressed replay keys and golden replay on model upgrade; unlocks C2/C5.
4. Heuristic hooks and in-tree advisors — Magellan vs MLGO still unsettled; unlocks C1.
5. Dialect/ISA feedback sinks — missing first-class surfaces from sim/silicon failures to proposals; unlocks C9/C10.
