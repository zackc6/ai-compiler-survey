## Key contributions

Uses 74 paired kernel problems across CuTe DSL and TIRx to study transfer of distilled optimization knowledge between languages. Across six models, paired improvement spans −19% to +29%, with a meaningful fraction of negative transfers.

## Summary

Retained memory or skill instructions are not automatically beneficial and do not themselves demonstrate a changed controller algorithm.

## Key takeaways

Report the baseline, hardware, evaluated object, and checker scope alongside any numeric result. Do not equate kernel speed with application throughput or optimization cost.

## Why it matters for this survey

Compare memory-only, distilled skills, learned policies and modified controller procedures with total source-experience cost; pair with [Meta-Harness](meta-harness.md).

## Limits / caveats

Same-problem cross-language transfer is not transfer to unseen workload classes or new accelerators. Source-attempt cost is extra.
