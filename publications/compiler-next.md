## Key contributions

- Vision of SE 3.0 intent/FMware compilation
- Treats prompts, agents, and free parameters as compile targets
- Lists 10 community calls to action

## Summary

Position paper arguing future compilers must search over multi-objective spaces that include LLM prompts and agent configurations, with quality gates and reproducible traces.

## Key takeaways

- Broadens 'compilation' beyond binaries
- Useful checklist for FMware tooling gaps
- Still mostly agenda-setting vs shipping system
